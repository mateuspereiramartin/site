Outreach

University and society

Outreach goes both ways: the university shares methods and knowledge with companies and institutions, and learns from the concrete challenges they face. This exchange improves decisions in practice and keeps teaching and research connected to society's real problems.

Partnerships

Applied research in partnership

Many organizational challenges cannot be solved with off-the-shelf formulas: they call for tailored decision-support systems with measurable results. In R&D projects with companies, we seek to turn operational bottlenecks into mathematical models and algorithms, and the questions that arise along the way can become research topics and classroom cases.

My most recent research projects started from needs in steelmaking, the aeronautics industry and air transport serving the oil and gas sector. Before my academic career, I interned at a mathematical modeling consultancy that applies operations research for companies across many sectors.

Areas

  • Production planning, scheduling and control
  • Raw-material cutting and yield of sheets, coils and bars
  • Scheduling of logistics operations, yards and fleets
  • Demand forecasting and inventory management

Training

Short courses

Course proposals that can run in a hybrid format or on site at the company. Content and length are adjusted to each request, and offering a course requires approval from the department and the university.

24 – 48 h

Demand forecasting and analytics

Time-series forecasting with Python for industry: forecasting methods, error metrics and using forecasts in planning.

24 – 48 h

Generative AI for operations

Using large language models (LLMs) as assistants in production planning and control: analyzing plant data, value-stream diagnostics, good practices and limitations.

24 – 48 h

Inventory management and DDMRP

Fundamentals of Demand Driven MRP: buffer positioning and sizing, and review of supply parameters, to protect material flow from variability.

24 – 48 h

Tactical planning: S&OP

Structuring Sales and Operations Planning (S&OP) to align forecast demand with production capacity and improve service levels.
