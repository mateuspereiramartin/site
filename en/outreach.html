Applied research for companies

Real problems need more than theoretical formulations: they need decision-support systems with measurable results. I translate operational bottlenecks into tailored mathematical models and algorithms, through R&D projects and university partnerships.

Before my academic career I worked at UniSoma, a mathematical modeling consultancy that applies operations research for companies in many sectors. Recent projects started from needs in steelmaking, the aeronautics industry and air transport for the oil and gas sector.

Areas

  • Production planning, scheduling and control
  • Raw-material cutting and yield of sheets, coils and bars
  • Scheduling of logistics operations, yards and fleets
  • Demand forecasting and inventory management
